# Privacy

OneShot Redux runs entirely in your browser.

- **No data collection.** It has no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
- **Nothing is uploaded.** Screenshots, text detection and card rendering all happen on your computer.
- **What it reads, and when.** Only when you start a capture (toolbar button or keyboard shortcut),
  it reads the current page's title, address, publish date, theme color and icon so it can label your card.
- **Network use.** The only network request it makes is fetching the current site's icon, from that
  site, at the moment you capture.
- **Storage.** Your style preferences, your recent captures and your Library (the cards you copy
  or download, with their text and source links) are kept in your browser's extension storage on
  your computer. You can delete cards at any time, turn off saving, or limit how many are kept; removing the
  extension deletes everything.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
